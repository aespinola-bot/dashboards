About this report. Type any U.S. street address above and click Generate report. The dashboard will geocode the address, detect the county and township via the US Census Geocoder, then render a 17-section due-diligence report — verdict tiles, location profile, traffic, schools, property taxes (county-specific for major PA counties), mortgage math, insurance, PFAS contamination flag (only for Horsham, PA), other environmental hazards, utilities, crime, commute, HOA & zoning, the full inspection package, PA-side closing costs, resale outlook, and a printable buyer checklist. Nothing is sent to a server we control — geocoding goes straight to the Census Bureau and OpenStreetMap. Your last-used address is stored in this browser's localStorage so the report restores on revisit.
Powered by US Census Geocoder · OpenStreetMap · FEMA · EPA · public-source intelligence. Not legal, tax, financial, or engineering advice — verify every figure and consult licensed professionals before any transaction.
