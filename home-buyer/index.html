🧭 Buy-side checklist

  1. Confirm assigned schools with the district (boundaries can be re-drawn).
  2. Check property tax: search "[county] property tax search" — Pennsylvania, Texas, NY, IL, NJ tend to be highest.
  3. Pull commute time: Google Maps "Directions" from the address to your office at the time you'd actually leave.
  4. Run a flood/wildfire check: FEMA Flood Map · FEMA NRI.
  5. Crime: county sheriff or city PD CompStat data; SpotCrime for a rough heatmap.
  6. Walkability: Walk Score.
  7. Listing comps: Zillow, Redfin, Realtor — sort by recently sold within 1 mi.
