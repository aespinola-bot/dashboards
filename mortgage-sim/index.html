Behavior modifier: Excellent −0.125%, Good 0%, Fair +0.250%, Poor +0.625%. PMI applied when LTV > 80% on conventional, ranging 0.30%-1.50% of loan/yr based on FICO & LTV.

Property tax & insurance state defaults

Lookup uses 2025 average effective property tax rates by state. Always verify with the county assessor and a real insurance quote.

DTI thresholds

Conventional Fannie Mae caps back-end DTI at 45-50% with compensating factors. FHA allows up to 56.99%. This simulator uses 43% as the conservative qualified-mortgage threshold; 28% front-end is a borrower comfort guideline (not a lender hard cap).

Disclaimer: This is an educational estimator. Actual rates & eligibility depend on lender overlays, loan program (FHA/VA/USDA/Jumbo), reserves, property type, and full underwriting. Always get a Loan Estimate from a licensed lender.
